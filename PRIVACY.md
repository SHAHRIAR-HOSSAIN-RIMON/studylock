# StudyLock Privacy Policy

*Last updated: 10 November 2026*

StudyLock is a Pomodoro timer and site blocker browser extension.

## What data StudyLock stores
- Your timer settings (session lengths, options)
- Your list of blocked websites
- Your focus statistics (number of sessions and minutes per day)

All of this is stored **only on your device**, using the browser's built-in extension storage.

## What StudyLock does NOT do
- It does not collect, send or sell any personal data.
- It does not track your browsing history. It only checks whether a page you open is on *your* block list, inside the browser, while a focus session is running.
- It has no account, no server, no analytics and no ads.

## Permissions and why they are needed
| Permission | Why |
|---|---|
| Access to websites (`<all_urls>`) | To redirect sites on your block list to the "stay focused" page during a focus session |
| `declarativeNetRequest` | The browser's built-in, privacy-friendly way of blocking sites |
| `storage` | To save your settings and stats on your device |
| `alarms` | To end sessions on time even when the popup is closed |
| `notifications` | To tell you when a session or break ends |

## Deleting your data
Uninstalling StudyLock deletes all of its data. You can also clear your stats at any time from the settings page.
